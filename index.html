05 // Studio Background & Pedagogy

Why Applied Linguistics is the Ultimate Edge in Instructional Engineering.

PASTEL (Pragmatic Applied Systems & Training Engineering Lab) is built on a foundational premise: mastering a complex fintech platform or enterprise software ecosystem is closely analogous to acquiring a specialized language. Traditional instructional designers rely on static templates; PASTEL approaches technical training through the lens of psycholinguistics, cognitive load theory, and communicative task design.

Backed by an M.S.Ed. in Applied Linguistics / TESOL and over a decade of rigorous curriculum architecture, our studio translates intricate system syntax into structured, digestible cognitive schemas. By replacing passive documentation with active task-based interaction, feedback loops, and input simplification, we reduce friction and design learning experiences aimed at measurable operational fluency.

PASTEL plans evaluation with the Kirkpatrick Four Levels (Reaction, Learning, Behavior, and Results), working backward from the business result a client wants. The portfolio modules above use fictional data, so they demonstrate design and instrumentation rather than measured results. Each has a case file with a design review: see the standard process.
